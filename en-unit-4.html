2 · Pơma atŭm

Dialogue

Dreng and Pôm talk after school.
Dreng
Dang ei dôm jơ boih?
What time is it now?
Pôm
Dang ei puơ̆n jơ boih.
It's four o'clock now.
Dreng
‘Năr âu jĭ ‘năr kiơ?
What day is it today?
Pôm
‘Năr âu jĭ ‘Năr pơ'dăm. Dơning jĭ ‘Năr tơdrâu.
Today is Friday. Tomorrow is Saturday.
Dreng
Dơning e hơ̆m bô̆k pơxrăm?
Are you going to school tomorrow?
Pôm
Êu, dơning ĭnh gô bô̆k pơxrăm ah pơgê. ‘Năr Bă Yang ĭnh pơdei.
Yes, tomorrow I will go to school in the morning. On Sunday I rest.
Dreng
Yơ̆ng bri ĭnh xang bô̆k tơ kơchơ păng mĕ.
Yesterday I went to the market with Mother.

Read the Bahnar line first, then check the English. Practise with a partner.

3 · Grammar

Grammar

1

Days of the week

Days are Năr + a number. Monday is “day one”.

‘Năr mônhMonday
‘Năr 'barTuesday
‘Năr pêngWednesday
‘Năr puơ̆nThursday
‘Năr pơ'dămFriday
‘Năr tơdrâuSaturday
‘Năr Bă Yang / ‘Năr giĕngSunday
2

Verbs do not change

A Bahnar verb has one form. Time is shown by time words (dơning, yơ̆ng bri) and small markers: xang … boih (already), gô (will), 'nao (just), tam (not yet).

Ĭnh xang tơl 20 xơnăm.
I am already 20 years old.
Dơning ĭnh gô năm hơpong kơ ih.
Tomorrow I will come to visit you.
Ĭnh 'nao xŏng.
I have just eaten.
3

Asking the time

Ask Dang ei dôm jơ boih? Answer with a number + jơ. Use ah for “at”.

Dang ei tơhngam jơ boih.
It's eight o'clock now.
ah pơgê hrôih
early in the morning

🔊 Sounds

x is said like English s: xơnăm, xang. j is like English j in “jam”: jơ, jĭ.

🏡 Culture note

The word khei means both “moon” and “month”, because the old Bahnar calendar followed the moon and the farming year. Sunday is ‘Năr Bă Yang, “God's day”, a name from the Christian communities of Kon Tum.
