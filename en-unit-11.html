2 · Pơma atŭm

Dialogue

Pôm and Dreng look at the sky.
Pôm
‘Năr âu tô̆ jat!
It's very hot today!
Dreng
Êu, plĕnh tơdah, uh kơ 'dei hơmơ̆l.
Yes, the sky is clear. There are no clouds.
Pôm
Dơning hơ̆m 'mi dĭ?
Will it rain tomorrow?
Dreng
Băt 'mi, băt uh kơ 'mi.
Maybe it will rain, maybe not.
Pôm
Ĭnh wă pơyan 'mi. Pơyan phang tô̆ jat.
I like the rainy season. The dry season is very hot.
Dreng
Ĭnh wă pơyan tơngĕt.
I like the cold season.

Read the Bahnar line first, then check the English. Practise with a partner.

3 · Grammar

Grammar

1

Describing without jĭ

With adjectives, you do not need jĭ: noun + adjective is a full sentence.

Pơgê âu plĕnh tơdah 'lơ̆ng.
This morning the sky is nice and clear.
'Dak lơ̆m mŏng âu tô̆ jat.
The water in this cup is very hot.
2

uh kơ 'dei = there is no

Uh kơ 'dei hơmơ̆l.
There are no clouds.
3

băt … băt = maybe … or

Use băt twice to show two possibilities.

Băt hăp lôch, băt erih.
We do not know whether he is alive or dead.

🔊 Sounds

ng can start a word in Bahnar: practise 'ngam (sweet) and ngua. Say “sing” and keep the ng without the “si-”.

🏡 Culture note

The Central Highlands have two main seasons: the rainy season, roughly May to October, and the dry season, roughly November to April. Farm work follows these seasons: fields are planted when the first rains come ('mi rah, the first rain of the season).
