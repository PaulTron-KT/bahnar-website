To the learner

Hmach kơ iĕm! Welcome.

This book is for adults and teenagers who are starting to learn Bahnar and who know English. It is also for Bahnar speakers who want to teach their language to others, or to learn English through their own language.

These goals match level A1 of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), the first level for beginners.

After finishing the book, you should be able to:

greet people, introduce yourself and talk about your family
count, tell the time and talk about days of the week
ask and answer simple questions: who, what, where, how many, how much
say what you want, what you can do, and what hurts
understand short, slow conversations about familiar topics

About the Bahnar language

A language of the Central Highlands

Bahnar (Ba Na) is spoken by about 287,000 people in Vietnam (2019 census), mainly in the provinces of the Central Highlands and the coast nearby: Kon Tum (present Quảng Ngãi), Gia Lai (including former Bình Định) and Đăk Lăk (former Phú Yên). It belongs to the Bahnaric branch of the Austroasiatic family, so it is related to Khmer and to many languages of the Central Highlands, but not to Vietnamese.

Bahnar has been written in the Latin alphabet since the second half of the 19th century. Catholic missionaries in Kon Tum, among them Fr. Pierre Dourisboure, made the first dictionary (Dictionnaire Bahnar – Français, 1889). This book follows the spelling of the Kon Tum Bahnar used in the 2008 dictionary, with the breve (˘) for short vowels.

There are several Bahnar dialects. Words and pronunciation can differ from village to village. If your teacher or your Bahnar friends say a word differently, follow them: they are the best guide.

Chư păng nơ̆r

Letters and sounds

Bahnar is written with Latin letters. Most letters sound much like they do in Vietnamese; the English comparisons below are only approximate. Always listen to Bahnar speakers. Bahnar has no tones. In words of two syllables, the last syllable is stressed, and the first one (often pơ-, tơ-, kơ-, hơ-) is short and light.
